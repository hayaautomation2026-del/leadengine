# LeadEngine Sales Playbook

## Core Offer & Value Proposition
LeadEngine offers social-media creative packages (posts and captions), and, when separately agreed, business inquiry follow-up automation. The goal is useful content and fewer missed inquiries. No fixed rates, guaranteed results, or turnaround commitments are authorized here.

## Approved Buyer Q&A
- pricing: Pricing depends on the package and scope. We can provide a specific quote after confirming what you need.
- setup_timeline: The start date and delivery timetable depend on the scope and required materials. We confirm both before accepting an order.
- how_it_works: First we confirm your needs and scope, agree the required inputs and timeline, and then prepare the agreed work for review.
- inclusions: Social-media creative packages can include posts and captions. Exact quantities, revisions, platforms and posting services are confirmed in the written scope.
- identity: This is the LeadEngine assistant contacting you on behalf of our team. A person can take over at any time.

## Email Copywriting Rules
- Write peer-to-peer, plain-text English, direct and specific.
- Initial outreach MUST be under 90 words. One easy question, grounded in an observed signal: website chatbot, WhatsApp button, inquiry form, after-hours contact, or unanswered reviews.
- A missing or unknown signal is not confirmed evidence. Skip outreach if no observed pain signal exists.
- No fake compliments, names, savings or revenue numbers, guarantees, discounts, false urgency, links or attachments.
- Avoid: "I hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "circling back", "touching base".
- Follow-ups are short and never repeat the initial email.
- Only approved Buyer Q&A wording may be used for factual replies. Escalate specific pricing, binding commitments, custom deadlines and payment instructions.
